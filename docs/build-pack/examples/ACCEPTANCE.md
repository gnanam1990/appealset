# AppealSet · Demonstration script

**Input and expected outcome.** Two initial reviews cost $0.10 each and an expert costs $0.30. With a $0.50 cap, labels A and B route to one expert. The expert accepts B and QA exports one patch with three payable entitlements totaling $0.50. With a $0.40 cap the first two reviews remain payable; the case waits for expert funds and exports nothing.

**Operator surfaces.** Steward dispute queue shows counts, taxonomy revision, age and funds. Reviewer workbench shows only the item, policy, due time and pay terms. Expert screen unlocks anonymized rationales after blind submissions close. QA export clearly differentiates concordant and expert decisions and excludes abstentions.

**Failure to demonstrate.** Atomically allocate slots and reserve spend. Expire unaccepted leases; reassign under policy and within budget. Idempotently finalize each submission and payable entitlement. When a payout status is unknown reconcile by assignment/remote reference. Append superseding decisions for corrected rulings; immutable prior export records remain reproducible.
